Rubric reference & calculation

Story · 5 / 5 / 90

Headline: Catchy phrase.

Subheadline: Complete sentence; avoids repeating information.

Copy: Past tense; LQTQ; overview or unique perspective. People are not featured elsewhere on the spread. Meaningful quotes explain why or takeaways without repeating facts. Factual transitions, no editorializing, proper grammar, and target word count.

Captions · 5 / 10 / 5 / 15 / 15 / 50

Photo selection: Well exposed; faces visible. Include photo by and file name.

A: Makes sense with the photo and caption.

B: Present tense. Who, what, when, where; describes the photo.

C: Past tense. How; information not visible in the photo.

DQ: Why they did it or enjoyed it; ends with “name said.” Does not repeat B or C. “Favorite” question: −50 points.

Mods · 5 / 60 / 30 / 5

Title: Short and catchy.

Captions / copy: ABCDQ captions OR 3–4 sentence quotes ending with “name said while in/at…”

Graphics / photos: 30 points.

Photo bys: 5 points.

How the average works

Sum of completed item totals ÷ completed item count. Each item has equal weight. Page average includes the story and completed captions; caption average excludes the story. Mods are calculated separately.

All category fields must contain valid scores for an item to count. Zero is a score. Partial rows are excluded and flagged. Results display to two decimal places; calculations use unrounded values.

One set of scores per item.
